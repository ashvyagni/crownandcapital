# Art Direction

## Thesis
Make governance feel tactile: parchment-like information surfaces, inked boundaries, restrained heraldry, and luminous state accents. The map is a readable instrument first and a decorative illustration second.

## Visual principles
- Strong hierarchy: realm status first, map second, action consequence third.
- Warm paper neutrals contrasted with deep ink and a small set of semantic colors.
- Ornament frames information; it must not reduce contrast or hit area.
- Use texture sparingly and preserve crisp text at responsive sizes.

## Implemented vs planned
The current build uses original illustrated city-world/deed SVGs, six traders, dealer/banker portraits, procedural 2D architectural marks and layered atlas motion. Fraunces and DM Sans are loaded under checked OFL licenses; `ASSET_CREDITS.md` records sources. `art-direction.css` is the final visual layer across landing and game, not just a marketing-page theme. Commissioned map painting, frame-animated sprites, faction heraldry and a scored soundscape remain future art work. Reference-inspection limits and real screenshot findings are recorded in `VISUAL_QA.md`.

## Accessibility
Color is never the sole status channel. Pair it with labels, icons, pattern, or text and maintain readable contrast.
